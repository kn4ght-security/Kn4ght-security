Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains what kn4ght security collects when it's added to a Discord server, and how that data is used.

What we collect

  • Discord user and server IDs, needed to apply moderation actions and settings per server.
  • Account metadata used for alt detection — account creation date, avatar presence, and join timestamps.
  • Moderation records: warnings, mutes, kicks, and bans issued through the bot, along with the moderator and reason given.
  • Server configuration you set, such as verification method and log channel.

What we don't collect

kn4ght does not read, store, or log message content outside of commands explicitly directed at it. We do not sell data to third parties.

Retention

Moderation logs are kept for as long as the bot remains in your server, so history stays available to your mod team. Removing the bot from a server schedules deletion of that server's data.

Your rights

Server owners can request export or deletion of their server's data at any time by contacting support.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support server linked in the footer.
