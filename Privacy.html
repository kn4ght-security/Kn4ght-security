Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of the information kn4ght security processes and how it is used.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information kn4ght security may process when the bot is added to or used in a Discord server, why that information is used, and the choices available to server owners and members.

2. Information we process

To provide security, verification, configuration, and moderation features, the bot may process:

  • Discord identifiers: user IDs and server IDs, which help the bot identify members and keep settings separate for each server.
  • Account and join information: account creation dates, whether an account has an avatar, and join timestamps, where available and used for alt or raid detection.
  • Moderation records: warnings, timeouts/mutes, kicks, and bans made through the bot, including the moderator, affected member, timestamp, and any reason provided.
  • Server settings: configuration selected by server administrators, such as verification options, detection settings, moderator roles, and logging channels.

3. Message content

kn4ght security is not designed to read or store ordinary server conversations. It processes interactions and command information directed to the bot. If a feature is later added that requires processing message content, this policy should be updated before that feature is used.

4. How information is used

  • To provide verification, moderation, alt-detection, and raid-response features.
  • To save server configuration and keep moderation logs available to authorized server staff.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the bot against abuse or technical problems.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord as part of the platform's operation, and may be disclosed where required by applicable law or where necessary to protect the service and its users.

6. Data retention and deletion

Moderation records and server configuration may be retained while the bot is active in a server so that its features continue to work and staff can review relevant history. When the bot is removed, deletion of that server's stored data is scheduled. Backups or technical logs, if any, may take additional time to expire.

7. Your choices and requests

Server owners or authorized administrators may request access to, correction of, or deletion of server data through the bot's official support server. Please include the relevant server ID and explain the request. Do not post passwords, bot tokens, or other secrets in a support request.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the bot. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please limit access to moderation logs and bot permissions to trusted people.

9. Children's privacy

Server administrators are responsible for ensuring that their use of the bot and their server complies with applicable age requirements, Discord rules, and local laws.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the bot's features or data practices change. The revised date at the top of this page indicates when it was last updated. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies to future use.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the kn4ght security team through its official Discord support server.
